Videos can now make sound, set words in style and star a stick figure, and the note box suggests what fits while you type.

- Sound effects: 186 sounds and 845 takes from earshot's bank (MIT, Leon Lin). Add one at any moment on an Audio lane; a whip cut brings its own whoosh and a flash brings a hit.
- Type: seven styles for words on screen: split-flap, typewriter, terminal, rubber, rack focus, letter cloud and editorial.
- A stick figure to direct: 9 faces, 6 mouth shapes, 14 poses, walks and runs, talks in time with the words, and holds props.
- Beat-cut reels: drop clips and a song and every cut lands on the beat, with whip and flash transitions.
- Suggestions while you type a note: looks, type styles, fonts, sound effects and music that fit. Tap one and its name goes into your note.
- What's new lives in the editor's header, with a dot when there's something new.
- A builder that needs a newer videobuilder can now raise its own version.
