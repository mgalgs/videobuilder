videobuilder can now do 923 more things by name, each with its own short demo, and the note box finds the right one for what you describe.

- 923 new capabilities in ten families: particles and generative, UI and interaction, typography, broadcast graphics, animation principles, visual effects, shapes and icons, transitions, camera moves and sound. Ask for one by name ("a dip to black", "an odometer roll") or describe it.
- The Capabilities page shows each family inside its category, with a page of its own. Every one also plays in the public gallery: https://mgalgs.io/videobuilder/
- Suggestions search every capability as you type a note. The picker has a search box, families fold into one row, and a strong suggestion says why it fits.
- New video is just a name. Your first note, typed in the editor, starts the build; the timeline's "+" plans a clip, acts, a song or a dropped video.
- Links in any note are read, and a linked page brings its images, colours and fonts, so "use this page's look" works. SVG images work as attachments.
- Delete a video from the Share tab; it goes to the trash and an admin can restore it.
- When a version fails to load, the editor says why and still lets you go back.
- A terminal log that types and prints with its own sounds, italic fonts, and faster renders for videos with lots of sound effects.
