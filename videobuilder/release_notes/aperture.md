A video can now be a film made of clips: each clip is rendered on its own, and a timeline.json cuts them together, with Audio lanes for music and sound.

- A note can target one clip. The builder sees its neighbours and the film's bible, so the clip still fits the film.
- A new New video screen: give it a name and, if you like, a project, then drop clips and a song, describe a short video, or plan a film in acts.
- The editor has a timeline: trim, dissolve or wipe between clips, J and L cuts, Audio lanes, reorder, and This clip or Whole video for each change.
- Filter looks work on any frame.
- Smaller cards in the library.
- Starter prompts read as quotes.
